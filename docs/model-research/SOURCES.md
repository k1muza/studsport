## Visual inspection scope

The six supplier drawing pages, Shenka catalogue photo pages 3–7 and 9, and Litai’s Canter M18/M20 × 78 product photograph were visually inspected. DT’s accessible specification table was used for dimensions; its image download failed, so this review does not claim a direct DT photo comparison. The Volvo depiction uses comparable headed-stud photographs for structure and marks local spline/transition geometry illustrative. Alibaba supplied leads but no independently verified technical drawing.

## Search and identification decisions

Search combinations included front/rear, M18/M20/M22 × 1.5, nominal lengths 47/72/75/78/90/91/102/105/108, LH/RH, and discovered manufacturer/OEM codes. Front 102 mm Nissan results were not used as rear-fitment evidence. Shenka CK10 rear BNTHNS0016 and BNTHNS0017 both have LH text in the source; a complementary RH part was not inferred. Mazda BNTHMZ0001 front/rear naming conflicts remain unresolved.

Before sourcing a physical product, obtain its supplier part number, drawing revision, both handed part numbers where offered, a defined length datum, tolerance chain, chassis/OEM fitment evidence and confirmed nut/seat pairing. This is an identification backlog, not a claim that all requested variants exist.
